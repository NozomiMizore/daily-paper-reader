## 摘要
从单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq）数据推断基因调控网络（GRNs）能够揭示细胞行为的内在机制，但缺乏时间信息以及普遍的缺失噪声使得这一任务充满挑战。为解决这些问题，我们提出了FlowGRN，一种将条件流匹配与得分匹配相结合的方法，用于稳健的轨迹重建，并联合dynGENIE3实现可扩展的GRN推断。FlowGRN引入了一种新颖的细胞相似性度量，该度量对高维scRNA-seq数据中的缺失效应具有鲁棒性。在BEELINE基准上的评估表明，FlowGRN在合成数据集和实验数据集上均达到了最先进的性能。消融研究验证了抗缺失相似性度量和轨迹重建步骤的重要性，凸显了FlowGRN准确建模动态调控关系的能力。

## Abstract
Inferring gene regulatory networks (GRNs) from single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data offers insights into cellular behavior, but is complicated by the lack of temporal information and the prevalence of dropout noise. To address these challenges, we present FlowGRN, a method that integrates conditional flow matching and score matching for robust trajectory reconstruction with dynGENIE3 for scalable GRN inference. FlowGRN incorporates a novel cell similarity measure that is resilient to dropout effects in high-dimensional scRNA-seq data. Evaluation on the BEELINE benchmark demonstrates that FlowGRN achieves state-of-the-art performance on both synthetic and experimental datasets. Ablation studies validate the importance of both the dropout-robust similarity measure and the trajectory reconstruction step, highlighting FlowGRN's ability to accurately model dynamic regulatory relationships.
