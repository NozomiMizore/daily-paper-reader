## 摘要
批次整合是单细胞基因组学中的一个核心预处理步骤，在此过程中，尽管存在普遍的技术性批次效应，来自不同实验、供体和方案的数据集仍须被合并。主流的整合方法会生成一个共享的低维嵌入，该嵌入舍弃了校正后的基因表达值，而下游的差异表达、生物标志物及其他分析都依赖于这些值。那些保留基因的特征空间（表达校正）方法通常对齐各批次的边缘表达分布，因此只要批次间细胞类型组成不同，即批次效应与生物学信号混杂，这些方法就有抹除真实生物学变异的风险。我们将单细胞批次整合重新定义为混杂域自适应，并应用 ConDo 方法，该方法在给定细胞类型注释的条件下匹配条件表达分布，而不是匹配边缘分布。为将 ConDo 的成对源-目标适配器扩展到多批次场景，我们引入了一种凝聚式兼容图整合器：批次作为节点，当它们共享某细胞类型时相连，我们通过拟合一个 ConDo 适配器，将得分最高的邻居贪婪地合并到一个不断增长的参考集中。在 Open Problems 批次整合基准测试中，ConDo 是最强的特征空间整合器，在六个数据集中的五个上，其在特征方法中排名第一。此外，在总分上它与深度嵌入方法相当或更优，在六个数据集中的四个上，其在所有方法中排名第一，同时返回校正后的表达值而非不透明的嵌入。

## Abstract
Batch integration is a central preprocessing step in single-cell genomics, where datasets collected across experiments, donors, and protocols must be combined despite pervasive technical batch effects. The leading integration methods produce a shared low-dimensional embedding, which discards the corrected gene-expression values that downstream differential-expression, biomarker, and other analyses depend on. The feature-space (expression-correcting) methods that do preserve genes typically align the marginal expression distributions of batches and thereby risk erasing genuine biological variation whenever cell-type composition differs across batches, i.e. whenever batch effect and biological signal are confounded. We recast single-cell batch integration as confounded domain adaptation and apply ConDo, a method that matches conditional expression distributions given the cell-type annotation rather than marginal distributions. To extend ConDo's pairwise source-to-target adapter to the many-batch setting, we introduce an agglomerative compatibility-graph integrator: batches are nodes connected when they share a cell type, and we greedily merge each best-scoring neighbor into a growing reference by fitting one ConDo adapter. On the Open Problems Batch Integration Benchmark, ConDo is the strongest feature-space integrator, ranking first among feature methods on five of six datasets. Furthermore, it is competitive with or better than deep embedding methods on the overall score, ranking first across all methods on four of six datasets while returning corrected expression rather than an opaque embedding.
